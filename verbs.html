Forms follow general Casablanca / Rabat Darija. Arabizi: 3 for ع, 7 for ح, ch for ش, gh for غ, kh for خ, q for ق (see docs/spelling.md). Future is ghadi plus the present without ka-. Negative is ma + verb + ch (ma ktebtch); the future is ma ghadich nkteb. nta and nti sound the same in the past.
